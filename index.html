MythLayer

A battlemap effects overlay for table-mounted displays. Pick a video map, choose the screen it lives on, launch the overlay. Effects render on top of the loop while your minis and terrain sit on the glass.
V0.21 polish + fire pass — added "flame mass" anchor blobs for big fires so the center reads as intense as the edges, hotspot size scales with fire size (huge fires now have proportionally larger flame tongues, not just more of them), all menu labels moved inward to fix LIGHTNING/FLAME overflow, pencil-eraser icon for CLEAR slices, DIAG removed from settings, welcome message stays 15 seconds.

Heads up: MythLayer uses your browser's multi-display API. Works best in Chrome or Edge. On the first launch your browser will ask permission to manage windows across your displays — say yes.
