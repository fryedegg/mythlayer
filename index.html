Made for touch. Built for your display.

MythLayer is software only. Touch features require a separate IR 10–20 point multi-touch frame/display or a capacitive multi-touch screen. Hardware is not included.

What can I do with touch? +

Calibrate your touch or IR setup to the table, then link digital effects to physical minis.

  • Pinch to resize placements.
  • Batch-link groups of minis.
  • Trigger scripted events when a mini crosses a configured zone.
  • Let controls face the active player without rotating the display.

Touch controls for table play are available now. Phone and tablet workflow improvements are planned through Sidecar; the playable website encounter demo follows broader touch readiness.
