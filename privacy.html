Website privacy & your choices

Updated October 18, 2026. This page explains analytics on mythlayer.com, the MythLayer marketing website. The downloadable MythLayer application has no analytics added by this website setup.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand where website visits come from and how people use the site. We measure the main pages and tabs, store and community link clicks, trailer engagement, public roadmap clicks and FAQ use. A store click is an expression of interest, not a recorded purchase.

The Google Analytics tag is loaded only after you choose Allow analytics. Until then, or when you decline, this site does not send Google Analytics events or set analytics cookies. The site continues to work either way.

What is collected

When analytics is allowed, Google processes analytics cookie identifiers, device/browser and approximate geographic information, and the website events above. We send controlled campaign labels and predefined page/content identifiers. FAQ analytics includes only question identifiers and broad query-length/result-count ranges; we do not send the words you type.

We remove unrelated URL parameters and full referring-page paths from our analytics event fields. We do not add advertising tags, session replay, user IDs, buyer email lists, payment exports or customer transaction records through this website code. Advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization remain disabled in the tag.

Google’s own processing is explained in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner sites.

Cookies, stored choices and withdrawal

We save your analytics choice in this browser’s local storage under mythlayer.website.analytics-consent.v1 for up to 180 days. This preference is saved when you choose either option. If analytics is allowed, Google’s _ga cookies are configured to expire after 180 days without extending their expiry on each visit. Your existing animation preference is separate.

You can change your choice using the website footer or the button above. Declining after previously allowing analytics disables further measurement, removes Google Analytics cookies accessible to this site and reloads the page to unload the tag. This does not erase events already processed by Google or cookies on other sites. Clearing browser storage also clears your saved choice.

Google Analytics event-level data retention is configured for 14 months. Standard aggregate reports can have different retention rules; the event-level setting does not delete every aggregate report after 14 months.

Links to itch.io, social sites and videos

Public campaign labels can follow a link to our itch.io page so its origin is not lost. When you allow analytics, Google may add a temporary cross-domain linker parameter to an itch.io link. The destination website controls its own analytics and privacy choices; your choice here does not automatically control itch.io, Discord or a social platform.

The native video clips are served as website assets. If you choose the embedded YouTube player, your browser connects to YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player. External services operate under their own privacy policies.

Hosting and contact

Porkbun continues to host this website. Hosting providers receive the network information needed to serve pages, independently of optional browser analytics. For questions about this website’s analytics, contact MJ through the MythLayer Discord.
