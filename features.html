The rest of your DM toolkit +

GPU-rendered spell effects and an effect brush. Fog of war, day/night lighting, rain, snow and storms. Accurate area templates, measurements and a built-in dice roller. A local music cue board with up to twelve named playlist buttons.

400+ creatures and 200+ player characters, custom monsters, D&D Beyond PDF import, encounter tools, session recaps and portable backups. LIVE AREAS shows who an effect touches; you handle saves and damage.

Touch controls & hardware +

Calibrate your setup, link digital effects to physical minis, pinch to resize placements, and turn the controls toward the active player without rotating the display.

MythLayer is software only. Touch features need a compatible IR 10–20 point multi-touch frame/display or capacitive multi-touch screen, purchased separately. Phone/tablet workflow improvements and the website encounter demo remain on the roadmap.

Browser & setup notes +

Use desktop Chrome or Edge. Firefox and Brave are not currently supported. Table play works with a TV or projector and a computer; a touch display is needed only if you want touch features. Remote sessions connect peer-to-peer with relay support; connection success can depend on network settings.
